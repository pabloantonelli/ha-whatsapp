# Code of conduct

## In short

Be respectful. This is a small hobby project maintained in spare time.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith, from maintainers and contributors alike.
- Keep discussion on the technical problem, not the person raising it.
- Accept that a feature request may be declined, and that a maintainer may be
  slow to answer.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, and publishing someone's
private information.

## Reporting

Report a problem by opening a private security advisory or contacting the
maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled discreetly.

The maintainer may edit, delete or reject comments, commits, issues and pull
requests that do not follow this document, and may block repeat offenders.
